10th Workshop on Visualization for the Digital Humanities

EuroVis 2027, Stuttgart

We are happy to announce that the 10th edition of VIS4DH — the Workshop on Visualization for the Digital Humanities — will take place as part of the EuroVis 2027 conference (31 May – 4 June 2027) in Stuttgart, Germany. After nine editions at IEEE VIS, the workshop moves to EuroVis to be closer to where its most active community is based.

The VIS4DH workshop brings together researchers and practitioners from the fields of visualization and the humanities to discuss new research directions at the intersection of visualization and (digital) humanities research. Papers and invited talks from previous years can be found here.

VIS4DH'27 has no specific theme: we openly invite contributions related to visualization and the (digital) humanities, in two tracks — a Paper track and an Agora track for lab talks, exhibits and provocations. See the call for contributions for details.

Key dates

  • 12 March 2027: Paper deadline
  • 9 April 2027: Paper notification
  • 16 April 2027: Agora track deadline
  • 23 April 2027: Paper camera-ready deadline and Agora track notification

These dates are tentative and based on the deadlines of the main EuroVis conference.
