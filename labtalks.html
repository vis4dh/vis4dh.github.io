VIS4DH Labs

Since 2020, the VIS4DH workshop has included Lab Talks, a format where research groups working at the intersection of visualization and the digital humanities present their work or labs more generally in brief lightning talks. As a way to highlight relevant actors and activities in our field — and to facilitate exchanges between them — we feature short profiles of these groups.

To have your lab listed on this website (or for changes to existing entries), please get in touch by using this form. We will make selections that ensure geographic and disciplinary diversity. Lab Talks are also part of the Agora track at VIS4DH'27. For questions, please do not hesitate to email us at: vis4dh@gmail.com.
